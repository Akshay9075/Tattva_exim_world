Company Introduction

A premium export house for handcrafted Indian excellence.

Tattva Exim Global connects international buyers with carefully selected GI tagged handcrafted products from India. Our work is built around refined curation, disciplined quality checks, ethical sourcing, and elegant presentation.

From boutique retail collections to hospitality and interior sourcing, we help global partners access products that carry cultural value, material beauty, and commercial reliability.

Our Story

Born from respect for artisans, built for international standards.

Tattva was created with a simple belief: Indian craft deserves a global stage equal to its beauty. We work with artisan ecosystems and product specialists to translate heritage into collections that feel timeless, premium, and ready for discerning markets.

Our name reflects essence. Every product is chosen to carry the essence of its place, process, and maker while meeting the practical expectations of modern importers.
